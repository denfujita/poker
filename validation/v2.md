# v2 validation: exposed-card conditioning

Source SHA-256: `30195ac646d64d71b9ec5cb58895b1365d155c1c543163135a3f3725d9b060a4`.

The previous estimator treated opponents as entirely unknown even after the flop selection exposed one retained card. This version fixes each live opponent's public card in every sampled hand and removes all exposed cards, including those of folded players, from the unknown deck. Own discarded cards remain dead.

## Evidence

- Remote build-box probe: **293 candidate decisions, zero failed decisions**, with a 1 ms decision limit and zero time bank.
- A private decision-history audit identified 335 call decisions where the opponent's public cards already made the hero unable to win. Re-evaluating these decision-time states with v2 returned **zero equity in all 335**. Only this aggregate is published; private histories are excluded.
- Public synthetic regressions check exposed opponent nuts, hero nuts, exclusion of live/folded public cards and the own discard, and sampling without duplicate cards. Existing selection, split-pot, short-stack and deterministic-sampling invariants remain covered.

This validates the specific information-handling correction and tested runtime behavior. It does not establish an improvement against the tournament field. The 800-table report in `benchmarks/` measures v1 only. Unknown cards are still uniformly sampled; v2 does not implement action-conditioned ranges or an opponent discard model.
