- A replay found all 799 sampled decisions outside the new calculation's scope unchanged.
- The submission checker, 342 legality states, and 90 malformed-memory cases passed.
- A 1 ms, zero-time-bank probe reported zero failures in 100 candidate decisions.
- The required 200-table, three-player match against `check_or_fold` and `min_raiser` averaged **43.35 points** with **154 wins** and zero failed decisions across all bots.
